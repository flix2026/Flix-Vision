Common Questions

How do I get access?
Contact us directly. Once we add your username to the system you'll be able to log in and set your own 4-digit PIN on first use.
Do I need to pay again?
No. Access is a one-time arrangement. Your account stays active as long as you have it.
What is Node.js and is it safe?
Node.js is a free, open-source JavaScript runtime used by millions of developers worldwide. It's made by the Node.js Foundation and is completely safe. You can verify it at nodejs.org.
Does it work without internet?
No — an internet connection is required to browse titles (which come from the TMDB database) and to play content.
Can I use it on more than one device?
Yes, up to 3 devices simultaneously with the same account.
I forgot my PIN. What do I do?
On the login screen, click "Forgot your PIN?" and send a reset request. We'll reset it for you.
What content does this include?
Flix Vision is a media browser — it displays metadata (titles, posters, descriptions) from The Movie Database (TMDB). When you choose to play something, your browser connects directly to publicly available third-party embed services. We do not host, provide, or control any of that content.

Flix Vision is independent software distributed as-is for personal use. We are not affiliated with any streaming service, studio, or content provider.

All content metadata provided by The Movie Database (TMDB). Third-party streams are accessed directly by your browser and are not hosted or served by us.
